# English

## Bug Fixes

- The extension's family shelf now marks a book "new" when a family member shares it later on, matching the phone app. Opening the family shelf tab counts as seen; the mark clears itself after 24 hours.
- Someone who has left or been removed from the family can no longer mark a borrow as returned; only the two parties still in the family can.
- Books with a blank cover can be borrowed again. When a borrow request fails, the screen now says why (a pending request already exists, lending is turned off, the connection dropped) instead of doing nothing.

## New Features

- The create-family and join-family screens show the custom server address in use, so you know where your data goes. Nothing is shown on the official server.

## Performance

- Per-minute request limits are now enforced by a lighter mechanism; the limits themselves are unchanged (60 / 30 / 10 / 3 per minute depending on the action). The one visible difference: when you are blocked, the countdown starts at 60 seconds instead of the seconds left in the current minute.
  - **Self-hosters**: a small config change is needed — see step 5 of `worker/DEPLOY.md`. Without it the server keeps working with the old counting method. Official-server users are unaffected.
- Each member keeps their 20 most recent finished borrows (returned, rejected, cancelled); older ones are cleared automatically. Every member's history is counted separately, so nobody else's records push out yours. Pending and lent-out records are always kept, however many there are.
- Each member can have at most 20 pending borrow requests at a time; beyond that, cancel one or wait for a reply before sending a new one. Normal use never reaches it.
- When someone leaves the family, the finished records of books they borrowed from others are deleted; records of books others borrowed from them stay. Books still out on loan stay until someone marks them returned.

## Security & Privacy

- Borrow records are only sent to the two parties involved; family members with no part in a borrow no longer receive it. The screen already showed only your own records, so nothing looks different.
- Book title links on the family shelf and public shelves only ever point to Readmoo (`readmoo.com` / `readmoo.tw` and their subdomains); anything else loses its link and shows the title alone.
- Protection against malformed data from a custom server now also covers the member list, member settings, borrow notifications and the family shelf's book list; entries in the wrong shape are ignored. Official-server users are unaffected, and nothing looks different day to day.

# 繁體中文

## 問題修正

- 擴充功能的家庭書櫃現在也會把家人後來才分享的書標成「更新」，和手機版一致。打開家庭書櫃分頁就算看過，標記會在 24 小時後自動消失。
- 已經離開或被移除的成員不能再把借閱紀錄標成已歸還，只有還在家庭裡的借閱雙方可以。
- 書封空白的書現在也能申請借閱了。申請失敗時畫面會說明原因（例如已有待處理的申請、對方關閉了借閱功能、連線失敗），先前按了沒有任何反應。

## 功能新增

- 建立或加入家庭的畫面會標出目前使用的自訂伺服器位址，讓你知道資料會送到哪裡。使用官方伺服器時不會顯示。

## 效能改善

- 防止有人瘋狂連線的每分鐘次數上限換了一套更省資源的做法，能用的次數完全沒變（依操作不同，每分鐘 60／30／10／3 次）。唯一看得到的差別：被擋下時畫面上的倒數都會從 60 秒開始，先前顯示的是該分鐘剩下的秒數。
  - **自己架設伺服器的人**：設定要改一下，做法在自行架設說明（`worker/DEPLOY.md`）的第 5 步。不改也能繼續運作，會沿用原本的計次方式。使用官方伺服器的人不受影響。
- 每個人自己申請的借閱，會保留最近 20 筆已完成紀錄（已歸還、已拒絕、已取消），更早的自動清掉；家人的申請各自計算，不會擠掉你的。待處理與出借中的紀錄不論幾筆都完整保留。
- 同時待處理的借閱申請每人最多 20 筆，超過時要先取消或等對方回覆才能再送新的。一般使用不會遇到。
- 有人離開家庭時，他向家人借書的已完成紀錄會一起刪除，家人向他借書的紀錄則會留著。還借在外面、還沒歸還的也會留著，直到有人標記歸還。

## 安全與隱私

- 借閱紀錄只會送到借閱雙方的裝置，跟這筆借閱無關的家人不會再收到。畫面本來就只顯示自己的紀錄，日常使用感覺不到差別。
- 家庭書櫃與公開書櫃裡的書名連結只會連到 Readmoo（`readmoo.com`／`readmoo.tw` 及其子網域），不是的話會拿掉連結、只顯示書名。
- 對「伺服器回傳怪資料」的防護擴大到家庭成員列表、成員設定、借閱提醒與家庭書櫃的書單，格式不對的項目會被忽略。使用官方伺服器的人不受影響，日常使用感覺不到差別。
